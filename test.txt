BUG 06 – Groomer Reassignment Allowed After Service Completion
Appointment ID: #132

Actual Result:
•⁠  ⁠After all service milestones are completed, the system still allows the admin to reassign the service to another groomer.
•⁠  ⁠The groomer assignment dropdown remains enabled.
•⁠  ⁠The admin can select another groomer even though the service has already been completed.
Expected Result:
•⁠  ⁠Once all service milestones are completed, the system should not allow groomer reassignment.
•⁠  ⁠The groomer assignment dropdown should be disabled after service completion.
•⁠  ⁠The completed appointment should retain its original groomer assignment.
