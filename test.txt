When a customer applies both a coupon and a promotion code, if the combined discount exceeds the original service price, subtract the original service amount from the total discount and display the remaining excess amount in a popup notification.

**Example:**
- Original Service Amount: $78
- Combined Coupon + Promotion Discount: $81
- Excess Amount: $3

**Expected Behaviour:**
1. Deduct the original service amount ($78) from the combined discount ($81).
2. Display the payable service amount as $0.
3. Show a popup notification indicating the excess discount amount ($3).

**Popup Message:**
"The combined discount exceeds the original service amount by $3."

The excess amount should be calculated dynamically based on the original service amount and the combined coupon and promotion discounts.
